## Executive Summary

Sending volume dropped from ~4,500 emails/day (Feb 8-13) to ~1,500 emails/day (Feb 15-17) — a **67% decline**.

**Three interconnected root causes identified:**

1. **Lead Pipeline Exhaustion** — 78% of all leads have completed sequences, 10,544 leads are queued but waiting
2. **Campaign Completion Wave** — Multiple campaigns hit 95%+ completion simultaneously
3. **Inbox Capacity Constraint** — 245 inboxes with daily_limit=2 creates a hard ceiling of ~490 new contacts/day

**Verdict:** System is working correctly within constraints. Not a bug or failure.

## 5. Root Cause Analysis

### Primary Cause: Campaign Completion Wave

Multiple campaigns hit 95%+ completion between Feb 13-15:

| Campaign | Completion | Impact |
|----------|------------|--------|
| Campaign 7 (Warehouse) | 99.99% | STOPPED - 6,511 leads done |
| Retail Stores | 96.56% | Minimal sending |
| Van Life | 95.39% | Minimal sending |
| Solar | 94.72% | Winding down |

**Combined impact:** ~25,000+ leads finished sequences within days, eliminating their email volume contribution.

### Secondary Cause: Inbox Capacity Ceiling

The `daily_limit=2` per inbox creates a hard constraint:

- **Max new contacts per day:** 490
- **Leads waiting for Step 1:** 10,544
- **Days to clear queue:** 10,544 ÷ 490 = ~21 days

The system is correctly throttling to protect deliverability.

### Contributing Factor: Sequence Delays

2-3 day delays between sequence steps mean:
- Leads in sequence only receive emails every 2-3 days
- On any given day, only ~40% of in-sequence leads are due for an email
- Volume naturally spreads thinner as the lead pool shrinks
